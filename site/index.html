Your TV, from the sofa.

Sohva Remote is a quick, ad-free remote for Sony Bravia TVs. It talks to the TV over your home network only, so keys land instantly and nothing leaves the house.

Everything the remote on the sofa does, and more.

Built for a Sony KD-65XF9005 with Android TV. Other Bravias with IP control should work too.

On the remote

  • Fast keys. D-pad, volume and channels go over the Android TV Remote protocol, so a press lands in tens of milliseconds.
  • Touchpad. A toggle beside the D-pad turns it into a touchpad: swipe to move, tap for OK, and rest your finger after a swipe to keep moving.
  • Swipe to change channel. The card shows what's on; one tap jumps back to the last channel.
  • Every input and app. Inputs, installed apps with their icons, and the full channel list.
  • Phone volume buttons. They change the TV volume while the app is open.
  • Type and talk. Type or dictate into the TV's search, or say "kanava 3", "volume 20" or "Netflix", in Finnish or English.

Without opening it

  • Quick Settings tiles. Power, mute, guide and your first scene, from the notification shade.
  • Home-screen widget. Volume, channel and mute.
  • Mute during calls. The TV goes quiet when the phone rings and comes back after.

On a schedule

  • Scenes. TV on, a channel, input or app, and a volume: "Game time" puts the PS5 on at 30.
  • Scheduled scenes. Pick days and times; they act only while the TV is on.
  • Sleep timer. Fades the sound over a minute, turns the TV off, and puts the volume back next time.
  • Wake-up TV. On at a set time, to a channel and volume.

No ads, no paywall
Every feature is there from the first launch.
No account, no cloud
The phone talks to the TV on your network. Nothing goes to the internet.
No pairing again and again
Pair once with the PIN on the TV. If the TV gets a new address, the app finds it again.

Get the app

Sohva Remote is coming to Google Play. Until then, install the APK yourself, on Android 8.0 or newer.

  1. Allow remote control on the TV

    Settings › Network › Home network setup › IP control: set Authentication to "Normal and Pre-Shared Key" and Remote start to On.

  2. Install the APK

    Download it from the releases page on GitHub and open it on the phone, or build it yourself with ./gradlew assembleRelease.

  3. Pair with the PIN

    Open the app on the same Wi-Fi. It finds the TV; type the PIN the TV shows.
